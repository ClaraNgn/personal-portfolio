My Projects

I am someone who enjoys combining visual storytelling, content planning, and audience insights to connect with communities. Through my student-led campaigns, I have promoted events and supported fundraising through social media. These projects have strengthened my skills in creative communication, teamwork, and campaign analysis. Below are the two projects I want to focus on most: the Spring Festival Campaign and the Summer Charity Project.

Across three annual activations, the account grew to 245 organic followers and generated 3,533 likes from 60 sampled posts, averaging 58.9 likes per post. The campaign was created exclusively for students of Le Quy Don High School, and its school-specific content, Lunar New Year timing, and pre-order funnel helped it reach that intended school community. Engagement peaked in Tet 2024 with 1,876 likes, including 154 likes on the short film “Cameo’s Untold Story.” Because official Meta reach and impressions were unavailable, audience fit is inferred from the campaign’s school-only scope and public profile activity rather than verified demographic data.

Source: public data from @cameo.cado, collected September 27, 2026. No paid promotion; official Meta reach and impressions were unavailable.

Looking back

Creativity, guided by purpose.

The Spring Festival Campaign and Summer Charity Project showed me how social media can bring people together around a shared purpose. With Spring Festival, I helped connect a school community through seasonal storytelling and event promotion. With Em Thơ, I led social media communication in support of a fundraising initiative for children in need. Together, these projects strengthened my ability to plan content, collaborate with a team, and reflect on campaign results. They have shaped the way I approach marketing: understand the audience, communicate with intention, and keep learning from the response.
